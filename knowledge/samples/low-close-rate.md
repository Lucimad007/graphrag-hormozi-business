# Synthetic close-rate diagnosis

This is original sample text for local GraphRAG demos. It is not an excerpt from any copyrighted book.

## Problem

A business can generate many inbound leads and still fail to close. Lead volume is a poor diagnosis when the close rate is the weak metric.

## Metric

Close rate measures how many qualified conversations become customers. If leads are high and close rate is low, inspect the offer and the sales process before buying more traffic.

## Strategy

Clarify the offer so the buyer understands the outcome, the mechanism, and the next step. Improve sales follow-up so interested leads are not left unworked.
