# Offer clarity (synthetic)

Original sample text for GraphRAG evaluation. Not copied from a copyrighted book.

## Offer

An unclear offer makes buyers hesitate even after they request a call. The promise, the mechanism, and the next step must be obvious.

## Problem

Confusion about what is being bought shows up as stalled closes, not as a shortage of leads.

## Strategy

Rewrite the offer around a specific outcome. State who it is for, what changes, and what happens after they say yes.
